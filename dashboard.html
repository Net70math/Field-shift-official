Strategies to compare
How the demo score is calculated
  • Crop family alternation (up to 25 points): consecutive crops from the same family count against the score.
  • Legume included (up to 20 points): full points if at least one legume appears.
  • Water match (up to 25 points): compares general crop water categories with stated availability.
  • Pest and disease breaks (up to 15 points): penalizes the same crop repeated consecutively, including the current crop.
  • Priority alignment (up to 15 points): share of selected priorities the simplified rules can assess as addressed.

Unassessable criteria are excluded. If less than half the scoring points can be assessed, the score shows “Insufficient data.”
